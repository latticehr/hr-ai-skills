# Initial skill set

The first public release was promoted from the internal sandbox at commit
`44d980a2304df58a4346fe2aa3bf52a7b7872895`.

## Weekly Updates Workflow

`weekly-updates-workflow` turns notes and authorized evidence into individual or
team updates. It supports user-led, evidence-assist, evidence-first, roll-up,
and reminder workflows. Preserve the user's voice, use the organization's
actual Lattice update questions when available, and keep save and submit
actions distinct.

## Performance Review Drafting and Quality Check

`performance-review-drafting-quality-check` drafts or improves self, peer,
upward, and manager reviews using the exact review assignment structure. Its
core differentiators are review-period fidelity, evidence synthesis by
question, bias checks, rating/narrative consistency, and explicit validation
before submission.

## 1:1 Preparation

`one-on-one-preparation` produces a concise, prioritized agenda grounded in
recent authorized context. It adapts content to manager-to-report, upward, or
peer relationships and treats sensitive notes, incomplete history, and
recognition/feedback visibility carefully.

## Grow Track Starter Kit

`grow-track-starter-kit` guides People teams through architecture decisions
before drafting competencies. It preserves existing job and compensation
structures, recommends the simplest maintainable model, requires a design
checkpoint, and clearly separates competencies, goals, and promotion decisions.

## Company-Specific Manager Coach

`company-specific-manager-coach` is the broad coaching companion for feedback,
development, difficult conversations, prioritization, and team leadership. It
uses company guidance when available, separates evidence from interpretation,
avoids employee ranking, and leaves judgment and follow-through with the
manager.

Use the dedicated `one-on-one-preparation` skill when the primary deliverable
is an evidence-backed agenda. Use the manager coach when the user needs broader
reasoning, language, or next-step coaching.

## AI Fluency Culture Starter Kit

`ai-fluency-culture-starter-kit` helps People teams design responsible AI
fluency programs. It focuses on judgment, impact, learning, and role relevance
rather than usage volume, and can produce readiness questions, competencies,
manager practices, review prompts, guardrails, and rollout plans.

## Shared design language

Across the collection:

- Lattice MCP is used only when connected and authorized.
- Non-Lattice inputs remain valid fallback sources.
- Missing evidence stays visible instead of being fabricated.
- Sensitive data is minimized.
- Saves and submissions require explicit approval and successful tool results.
- AI supports human preparation and drafting; it does not make employment
  decisions.
